ShareX v1.0.4 brings the screenshot and offline OCR workflow to Linux and adds a universal macOS disk image. Pick the installer for your operating system.

## What's new

- **Linux desktop app.** Capture a screen, region, or window in a Wayland or X11 session, recognize text offline with Tesseract, and browse local screenshot history. Capture and shortcut support depends on the desktop session.
- **macOS disk image.** The same DMG works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Open it and drag **ShareX Mac.app** into Applications.
- **Windows download.** The EXE is the unmodified official ShareX 22.5.0 installer from the ShareX team. It has its own version and feature set. [Upstream release and checksum](https://github.com/ShareX/ShareX/releases/tag/v22.5.0) · [Windows source](https://github.com/ShareX/ShareX/tree/v22.5.0) · [Windows license](https://github.com/ShareX/ShareX/blob/v22.5.0/LICENSE.txt)
